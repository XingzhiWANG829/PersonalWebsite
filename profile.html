About

I am a Professor and PhD Supervisor in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Northwestern Polytechnical University. My research focuses on digital design, intelligent manufacturing and sustainable manufacturing, with particular interests in data-driven engineering design and AI-assisted engineering decision-making.

I received my PhD in Manufacturing Engineering and Management from UNSW Sydney and worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Melbourne in 2024–2025. My research experience also includes roles at Southern University of Science and Technology, Flanders Make, and the Singapore University of Technology and Design.
